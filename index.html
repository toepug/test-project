Eye

A longer eye (myopia) puts the focus in front of the retina; a shorter one (hyperopia), behind it.

0 D is distant (parallel) light; higher values are nearer objects, whose light diverges and focuses further back. With accommodation, the lens rounds up to pull the focus back onto the retina, up to its limit.
